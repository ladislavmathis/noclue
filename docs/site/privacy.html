noclue privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026. Applies to the noclue by ignition Chrome extension, version 0.4 and later.

noclue is a Chrome extension that helps you answer questions during online meetings. It has no server, no user accounts and no analytics. The developer of noclue receives none of your data.

What noclue processes and where it goes

DataWhenSent toStored
Audio of the meeting tab you chooseOnly while you are listeningOpenAI, for live transcriptionNever written to disk
Your microphoneOnly while you are listening, or during the microphone testNowhere. It is analysed on your computer only to detect when you speakNever
Recent meeting transcript (up to 5 minutes)When an answer is preparedOpenAI or Anthropic, whichever you chooseIn memory until you press Stop
Project facts, role, meeting goal and "About me" profile you enterWhen an answer is prepared or you try a practice questionOpenAI or Anthropic, whichever you chooseIn this Chrome profile (chrome.storage.local)
Your API keysWhen noclue calls the providerOnly to the provider the key belongs toIn this Chrome profile (chrome.storage.local)
Preferences, microphone choice, cue window positionNever sentNowhereIn this Chrome profile

Requests go directly from your browser to api.openai.com and, if you choose Anthropic, api.anthropic.com. They are made with your own API keys under your own agreements with those providers. Their policies apply to what they receive:

  • OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy and https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy
  • Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy and https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms

noclue asks OpenAI not to store answer requests (store: false). This does not guarantee that a provider keeps no data. Check your provider account's data retention settings.

What noclue does not do

  • It does not record or save meeting audio.
  • It does not send your microphone audio anywhere.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer data to anyone, and uses no data for advertising or creditworthiness.
  • It does not collect analytics, crash reports or usage statistics.
  • It does not read web pages other than the meeting tab you start it on, where it shows the optional cue window.

Test mode

If you turn on test mode, questions, answers and timing are kept in memory so you can export them as a JSON file. Nothing is exported automatically. Stop, Clear test or closing the extension removes the recording.

Your choices

  • Press Stop at any time to end listening and clear the transcript.
  • Delete your keys and project in Settings, or remove the extension to delete everything it stored.
  • Revoke an API key in your OpenAI or Anthropic account at any time.

Other people in your meeting

Transcribing other participants may require their consent under the law where you or they are. You are responsible for informing participants and obtaining consent where required.

Children

noclue is not directed at children under 16.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published in this file and noted in the changelog. Questions: open an issue at the project's GitHub repository or use the contact address in the Chrome Web Store listing.
