Not knowing is normal

No clue? No problem. Just install noclue by ignition.

noclue is a Chrome extension for Google Meet and Microsoft Teams. It listens to the meeting, notices a question and suggests one to three sentences you can say in your own words, based only on the facts you give it.

Add to Chrome How it works

Checked against your facts

Every factual sentence is checked before you see it. Unknown numbers stay unknown.

Your own key, no account

Use your OpenAI key, and Claude by Anthropic if you like. You pay the provider directly.

Your microphone stays here

It only tells noclue when you speak. There is no noclue server and no tracking.

How it works

  1. Install and connect. A three-minute guide asks for your API key, tests your microphone and explains where your data goes.
  2. Add a few facts. Your project, your role and what you want from the meeting. Short notes are enough.
  3. Start listening in the meeting tab. When someone asks you something, a short answer appears in the side panel or a small cue window.

Install

noclue is free and open source. It works in Chrome on the desktop, for meetings in English, Slovak, Czech or German in Google Meet and Microsoft Teams on the web. You need an OpenAI API key.
